Privacy Policy — Kundli AI

Last updated: July 22, 2026

Kundli AI ("we", "our", "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Kundli AI, we may collect:

  • Account information: Your email address, used to log you in via a one-time password (OTP).
  • Birth details: Name, date of birth, time of birth, and birth place (including geographic coordinates), which you provide to generate your Kundli (astrological birth chart).
  • Chat messages: Questions you ask our AI astrology assistant, along with the AI-generated responses.
  • Payment information: When you add money to your in-app wallet, payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not store your card, UPI, or bank details ourselves — these are handled directly by Razorpay under its own privacy and security policies.
  • Usage data: Basic technical information such as device/browser type, needed for the app to function correctly.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate your Vedic astrology birth chart, horoscope, and related readings.
  • To provide AI-powered astrology chat responses personalized to your birth details.
  • To manage your account, login, and in-app wallet balance.
  • To process payments for wallet top-ups.
  • To improve and maintain the app's features and reliability.

3. Third-Party Services We Use

To provide our features, we share relevant data with the following trusted third-party services:

  • Prokerala API — for Vedic astrology calculations (birth chart, planetary positions, dosha checks, panchang, matching). Your birth date, time, and location are sent to Prokerala to perform these calculations.
  • Anthropic (Claude AI) — for generating horoscope text and chat responses based on your astrological data.
  • Firebase (Google) — for authentication (login) and secure storage of your account data.
  • Razorpay — for processing wallet payments.
  • LocationIQ — for looking up birth place coordinates when you search for a location.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Firebase/Google Cloud infrastructure. We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and birth chart data for as long as your account is active, so you don't need to re-enter your details each time. You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 7).

6. Children's Privacy

Kundli AI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your Rights & Choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at the email below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
