Refund & Cancellation Policy — Kundli AI

Last updated: July 27, 2026

This policy explains how refunds and cancellations work for wallet top-ups and paid features on Kundli AI.

1. Wallet Top-Ups

When you add money to your Kundli AI wallet via Razorpay, the amount is credited to your in-app wallet immediately upon successful payment. Wallet top-ups are generally non-refundable once completed, except in the cases described below.

2. When Refunds Apply

  • Failed/duplicate payment: If money was deducted from your bank account/card but not reflected as wallet balance in the app due to a technical error, you are entitled to a full refund or wallet credit correction.
  • Service not delivered: If you were charged for a feature (e.g., a horoscope or AI chat question) but did not receive any output due to a system error, the amount will be credited back to your wallet automatically or on request.

3. When Refunds Do Not Apply

  • Once a horoscope, chat response, or Kundli matching result has been successfully generated and delivered, the associated charge is non-refundable, as the service has already been rendered.
  • Unused wallet balance is not eligible for cash refund to your bank account; it remains available for future use within the app.
  • Change of mind after receiving a reading/response.

4. How to Request a Refund

If you believe you are eligible for a refund under Section 2, please email us within 7 days of the transaction with your registered email, the approximate date/time of the transaction, and a brief description of the issue:

We aim to review and respond to refund requests within 5-7 business days. Approved refunds will be processed back to your original payment method via Razorpay, and may take 5-10 business days to reflect depending on your bank.

5. Cancellations

Since Kundli AI's paid features (horoscope generation, AI chat) are delivered instantly upon payment, there is no "cancellation" window after a request has been submitted and processed. You may simply choose not to use your remaining wallet balance for future requests at any time — there is no subscription or recurring charge to cancel.
